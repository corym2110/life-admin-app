# Future ideas (not v1)

Per the project brief, these are explicitly out of scope for v1 — not built, just parked here for later.

- **Cloud sync** — sync data across devices instead of on-device only.
- **Accounts / login** — required for cloud sync and household sharing.
- **Household sharing** — multiple people sharing the same item list.
- **OCR / AI photo scanning** — auto-extract due dates from a photo of a document/receipt.
- **Calendar sync** — push due dates into the phone's calendar app.
- **Email receipt forwarding** — forward a renewal email to the app to auto-create an item.
